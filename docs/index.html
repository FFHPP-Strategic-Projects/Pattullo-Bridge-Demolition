Pattullo Bridge Demolition

Application for a Fisheries Act Authorization (FAA) for the Pattullo Bridge Demolition (PBR) as submitted by the TRansportation Inventment Corporation (TI Corp; the Proponent).

Project Description

TI Corp is proposing the demolition of the Pattullo Bridge, which spans the Fraser River between New Westminster and Surrey. The existing structure is a steel truss bridge supported by a series of concrete piers, six (6) of which are located within the Fraser River. Proposed works include removal of three (3) in-water piers via blasting and removal of three (3) in-piers via conventional demolition methods.

To offset the proponent has proposed to construct new intertidal marsh habitat with a low flow channel and surrounding riparian vegetation at Cumberland Point (where the Brunette River discharges into the Fraser River), in New Westminster.
